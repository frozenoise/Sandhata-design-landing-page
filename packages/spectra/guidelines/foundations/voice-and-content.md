# Voice & content

Confident, plain, builder-to-builder. Short, active, benefit-first copy that
addresses the reader as "you." Applies to every generated string — button
labels, headings, helper text, error messages, empty states.

## Voice & tone

- **Benefit-first**: "A set of beautifully designed components that you can
  customise, extend, and build on," not "We provide components."
- **Second person**: "components you can customise" — avoid "we" and "our."
- **Calm and instructional**: helper text is neutral and descriptive, never
  cute or exclamation-heavy.
- **No emoji** — in UI or copy, ever. Not in button labels, not in empty
  states, not in error messages.

## Mechanics

- **Sentence case** for headings, buttons, labels, tabs — proper nouns only
  get capitals.
- **British spelling**: colour, customise, organise (not color, customize,
  organize).
- **Numbers**: grouped with commas (`24,828`), compact currency (`$0/mo`).
- **Errors**: direct, e.g. `"This is a mandatory field!"` — state what's
  wrong, not an apology.
- **CTAs**: verb-first and short — `Submit`, `Get started`, `View
  documentation`. Not `Click here to submit the form`.

## Applying this to generated UI

When writing placeholder or example copy for a generated screen, follow the
same rules a real Sandhata page follows — not generic Lorem Ipsum or
"Welcome!"-style copy. A generated dashboard heading should read like
`"Everything your team needs to ship consistent, accessible interfaces"`, not
`"Welcome to your dashboard! 🎉"`.
